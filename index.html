中文版本

我们重视你的隐私。守护钱袋子是一款本地风险提醒工具，用于在可能存在资金风险的操作场景中提醒用户先确认，再继续操作。

本应用当前版本不提供账号登录、不接入后端服务器、不进行云端同步。相关数据仅保存在用户设备本地。

一、我们处理的数据

为实现应用的核心功能，本应用可能在用户设备本地处理以下数据：

  • 紧急联系人姓名和手机号： 由用户手动输入或主动从通讯录选择，仅保存在本设备，用于在风险提醒时打开系统拨号界面。 本应用不会自动拨打电话。
  • 应用设置： 包括守护开关、语言设置、权限状态、无障碍增强模式等，仅保存在本设备，用于维持应用功能和用户偏好。
  • 前台应用状态： 在用户主动授权无障碍服务后，本应用可能在本机识别当前打开的应用类别，用于判断是否需要展示风险提醒。
  • 通话状态权限，可选： 用于辅助识别可能存在风险的操作场景。该权限仅用于本地风险判断，不会读取或保存通话内容。
  • 悬浮窗权限： 用于在必要时展示本地风险提醒弹窗。

二、我们不会做什么

本应用不会：

  • 上传紧急联系人信息。
  • 上传通讯录、短信、通话内容或屏幕内容。
  • 读取、记录或上传键盘输入、验证码、支付密码、银行卡号或银行账户信息。
  • 自动拨打电话。
  • 出售、共享、出租或向第三方提供个人数据。
  • 将本地数据用于广告投放、用户画像或商业营销。

三、无障碍服务说明

本应用使用无障碍服务的目的，是在用户设备本地识别当前打开的应用类别，以便在可能存在资金风险的操作场景中展示提醒。

无障碍服务仅用于本地风险提醒功能。本应用不会通过无障碍服务读取、保存或上传屏幕上的文字、密码、验证码、聊天内容、支付内容、银行卡信息或其他敏感内容。

在开启无障碍服务前，应用会向用户展示用途说明。用户可以选择开启或拒绝开启，也可以随时在系统设置中关闭该权限。

四、数据存储与删除

当前版本不提供后端服务，紧急联系人和应用设置仅保存在用户设备本地。

用户可以通过以下方式删除相关数据：

  • 在应用内修改或删除紧急联系人。
  • 在系统设置中清除应用数据。
  • 卸载本应用。

卸载应用或清除应用数据后，本地保存的数据会随系统规则被删除。

五、用户控制

用户可以随时：

  • 在应用内关闭守护功能。
  • 在系统设置中关闭无障碍、悬浮窗、通话状态等权限。
  • 修改或删除紧急联系人。
  • 卸载应用以停止所有本地功能。

六、未成年人使用

本应用不专门面向儿童。如未成年人使用本应用，建议在监护人指导下进行。

七、安全提醒

如果有人要求你卸载本应用、安装另一个同名或相似应用，或给陌生应用开启无障碍权限，请先联系可信联系人确认。 请不要向陌生人提供验证码、支付密码、银行卡信息，也不要按照陌生人的要求开启屏幕共享或远程协助。

八、隐私政策更新

我们可能会根据功能变化更新本隐私政策。更新后会在本页面展示新的生效日期。 正式发布后，请以应用内和官方网页展示的最新版本为准。

English Version

We value your privacy. Guard Wallet is a local risk reminder tool designed to remind users to confirm before continuing in situations that may involve financial risk.

The current version of this app does not provide account login, does not connect to backend servers, and does not perform cloud synchronization. Relevant data is stored only on the user's device.

1. Data We Process

To provide core features, this app may process the following data locally on the user's device:

  • Emergency contact name and phone number: Entered manually by the user or actively selected by the user from contacts. This information is stored only on the device and is used to open the system dialer during risk reminders. The app does not automatically place phone calls.
  • App settings: Including guard switch status, language settings, permission status, accessibility enhancement mode, and similar preferences. These settings are stored only on the device.
  • Foreground app status: After the user actively grants Accessibility Service permission, the app may locally identify the category of the currently opened app to determine whether a risk reminder should be displayed.
  • Call status permission, optional: Used to help identify situations that may involve financial risk. This permission is used only for local risk detection and does not read or store call content.
  • Overlay permission: Used to display local risk reminder pop-ups when necessary.

2. What We Do Not Do

This app does not:

  • Upload emergency contact information.
  • Upload contact lists, SMS messages, call content, or screen content.
  • Read, record, or upload keyboard input, verification codes, payment passwords, bank card numbers, or bank account information.
  • Automatically place phone calls.
  • Sell, share, rent, or provide personal data to third parties.
  • Use local data for advertising, profiling, or commercial marketing.

3. Accessibility Service Disclosure

This app uses Accessibility Service to locally identify the category of the currently opened app on the user's device, so that it can display reminders in situations that may involve financial risk.

Accessibility Service is used only for local risk reminder functionality. The app does not use Accessibility Service to read, store, or upload on-screen text, passwords, verification codes, chat content, payment content, bank card information, or other sensitive content.

Before enabling Accessibility Service, the app will display an explanation of its purpose. Users may choose to enable or decline the permission and may turn it off at any time in system settings.

4. Data Storage and Deletion

The current version does not provide backend services. Emergency contacts and app settings are stored only on the user's device.

Users may delete related data by:

  • Modifying or deleting the emergency contact within the app.
  • Clearing app data in system settings.
  • Uninstalling the app.

After the app is uninstalled or app data is cleared, locally stored data will be deleted according to system rules.

5. User Control

Users may at any time:

  • Turn off the guard feature within the app.
  • Disable Accessibility, overlay, call status, and other permissions in system settings.
  • Modify or delete the emergency contact.
  • Uninstall the app to stop all local functions.

6. Children

This app is not specifically directed to children. If minors use this app, we recommend that they do so under the guidance of a parent or guardian.

7. Safety Reminder

If someone asks you to uninstall this app, install another app with the same or a similar name, or enable Accessibility permissions for an unfamiliar app, please contact a trusted contact first to confirm. Do not provide verification codes, payment passwords, bank card information, or enable screen sharing or remote assistance at the request of strangers.

8. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as app features change. Any updates will be published on this page with a new effective date. After official release, please refer to the latest version displayed in the app and on the official webpage.
